# Mission

## Outcome
Describe the durable outcome this ecosystem exists to create.

## Beneficiary / user
Who should be better off if the system succeeds?

## Current hypothesis
What is the highest-leverage current belief about how to improve the outcome?

## Acceptance evidence
What evidence is sufficient to accept a claim of progress? Prefer direct outcome evidence; use mechanical ranking only where repeated optimization is appropriate.

## Constraints and authority
List protected invariants, spend/side-effect limits, and actions requiring human approval.

## Non-goals
State what this ecosystem should deliberately not optimize or automate.

## Stop / revisit conditions
State when the system should stop, escalate, or reconsider this mission/evaluator.
